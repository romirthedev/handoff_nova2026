What it does

01 · Tickets

One rail for every source.

Each ticket shows the guest, the platform, the courier and how long it's been waiting. Tap to fire, or just say it.

02 · Voice

"Start the timer for order 45 steak."

Timers set themselves from the dish and the doneness. Medium-rare ribeye gets 15 minutes. Ask how long is left, mark it ready, send it out.

03 · Pass camera

Green means go.

Slide the order under the phone. It names the guest, checks every item against the ticket, and flashes red with what's missing or extra.

Set up in a minute

01
Open your kitchen on the laptop at the pass. Tickets appear on the rail.
02
Scan the QR code on the right of the dashboard with your phone and tap Ready to identify. Set the phone on a shelf or stand, camera facing down at the packing spot.
03
Print the food cards from the print kit to demo without a kitchen: lay an order's cards under the camera, leave one out, and watch it catch it.
04
Turn on the mic and talk to the rail. Space also toggles listening.
