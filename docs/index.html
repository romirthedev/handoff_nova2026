The problem · DoorDash’s two challenges

Timing and packing break at peak.

01 · Prep timing

When should this order start cooking?

Orders arrive from different apps with different timing, and pickup times move. Start too early and food sits; too late and the Dasher waits.

How can AI help coordinate prep and timing?

02 · Packing & pickup

Is this bag right, and is it theirs?

Cooked isn’t done. Everything has to be packed right and handed to the right person, and every fix steals time from the next order.

How can AI make pickup more reliable, with less work for staff?

Our solution, put simply

One rail. Run by voice. Timed to the Dasher.

01 · One rail

Every app in one place.

DoorDash, Uber Eats, phone and walk-in orders on one live ticket rail.

02 · Fully by voice

No hands on a screen.

“New order for table 2…”, “what’s late?”, “start all the medium rare steaks.”

03 · Smart timers

Timers set themselves.

From the dish and the doneness: a medium-rare ribeye gets 15 minutes.

04 · Follows the courier

Start times update live.

Each ticket counts down to when to start, and moves when the Dasher runs early or late.
